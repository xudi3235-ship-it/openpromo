## Overview

We built OpenPromo to simplify content planning while respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal data across the login experience and in the app. By continuing to use OpenPromo, you agree to the practices described below.

## Information We Collect

- **Account details:** email, name, workspace information, and authentication metadata when you sign in.
- **Connected account tokens:** OAuth tokens and identifiers from platforms (e.g., Meta, TikTok) that you authorize.
- **Usage insights:** interactions within the dashboard, including scheduling actions, workspace settings, and preferences.
- **Support interactions:** messages and files you share with our team when requesting help.

## How We Use Data

- Deliver and secure the OpenPromo login dialog and dashboard.
- Power scheduling workflows, analytics, and notifications inside the app.
- Maintain compliance with connected platforms and monitor system health.
- Respond to support requests and improve product features.

## Login & Authentication

We use secure authentication providers for the login dialog. Credentials are never stored in plain text, and OAuth tokens are encrypted in transit and at rest. If you enable multi-factor authentication with a connected provider, their policies also apply.

## Sharing & Third Parties

- We share minimum required data with social platforms you connect for publishing and analytics.
- Infrastructure providers (e.g., hosting, logging, analytics) may process limited technical metadata under contractual safeguards.
- We never sell personal information.

## Data Retention & Deletion

We retain workspace and content data while your subscription remains active. You can request deletion of account data or revoke connected accounts at any time by contacting support. Backups and logs are purged on a rolling schedule consistent with regulatory and platform requirements.

## Security

OpenPromo uses encryption, access controls, and continuous monitoring to protect your data. Despite our efforts, no system is completely secure, so please notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

## Your Choices

- Update account information directly within the app.
- Disconnect social accounts through the dashboard at any time.
- Opt out of non-essential communications by using the unsubscribe link or contacting support.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product or regulatory changes. We'll notify users of significant updates through the app or email. Continued use of OpenPromo after changes become effective means you accept the revised policy. See `packages/www/src/pages/terms.mdx` for terms governing service use.

## Contact

Questions or requests? Reach us at [support@openpromo.app](mailto:support@openpromo.app).
